# Assets for this subpage

The current page uses the unchanged main `luffy-hero.webp`, `law-profile.webp`, and the more-covered `robin-profile-covered.webp`, generated as original fan illustrations with the built-in ImageGen tool. Their original PNGs are saved alongside their WebP copies. Robin's previous portrait and the small `luffy-bottle` source are retained but no longer appear on the page. These are not downloaded official artwork or asserted to be licensed One Piece assets. Character rights belong to their respective holders; no claim of official affiliation is made.

`mugiwara-skull.svg` is a project-drawn fan SVG of the straw-hat skull and crossbones, using the existing icon palette. `straw-hat.svg`, `pirate-ship.svg`, clouds, sea, bunting, and panel decorations are also project-created vector/CSS graphics.

The active frame, `f2-birthday-frame.png`, was supplied by the user as `frame-circular.png` and copied without editing its pixels. It is a 1080 × 1080 indexed PNG with a transparent centre and exterior, an ornate navy-and-gold ring, red ribbons, anchors, skulls, and Happy Birthday F2 lettering. The page previews and downloads this supplied PNG. No additional source or license claim is made for it.

`frame-circular.svg` and its 1024-pixel PNG export are the earlier unused concept, with a coral band, gold rope, cream curved lettering, straw-hat skull and compass badges. The old non-circular `frame-concept.svg` is also retained unused. The SVG concept remains editable and its render script targets the unused concept PNG, not the active supplied frame.

The fonts use Google Fonts with readable local fallbacks. Their source licenses are [Bangers](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/bangers/OFL.txt), [Caveat](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/caveat/OFL.txt), and [Nunito](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/nunito/OFL.txt), under the SIL Open Font License.
