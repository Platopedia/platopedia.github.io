# Assets for this subpage

`luffy-hero.png` and `luffy-bottle.png` were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on October 12, 2026 as original commissioned **Luffy fan illustrations**. The WebP copies preserve transparency and reduce the served images to approximately 557 KB combined. These are not downloaded official artwork or asserted to be licensed One Piece assets. The character belongs to its respective rights holders; no claim of official affiliation is made.

`straw-hat.svg`, `pirate-ship.svg`, `frame-concept.svg`, clouds, sea, bunting, and panel decorations are project-created vector/CSS graphics. The frame is visibly labelled as a concept until the official frame is supplied.

The fonts use Google Fonts with readable local fallbacks. Their source licenses are [Bangers](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/bangers/OFL.txt), [Caveat](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/caveat/OFL.txt), and [Nunito](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/nunito/OFL.txt), under the SIL Open Font License.
